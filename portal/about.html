How it works

The problem: complaints get filed, then nobody follows up. This system gives every complaint an owner, a time limit, automatic reminders and an escalation path, and keeps the citizen informed at each step.

The life of a complaint

RegisterThe citizen picks a category, adds photos and an address. The address is matched to a zone.
RouteFive tools classify it, look up its time limit, find the owning office and officer, and draft the reply.
Do the workA worker in that zone accepts it and does the job.
Close with proofThe worker asks for a completion code. It goes to the citizen, who confirms the fix.
RemindA check runs every 30 seconds. The officer gets reminders at 50% and 80% of the time limit.
EscalateIf the limit passes, the complaint moves up one level automatically, all the way to the DC Office.
Not fixed?The citizen can say it is not fixed. It goes back up, and the history is kept.
TrackThe citizen sees every step, and gets messages in English or Kannada.

Agents and models: what is used, and where

Principle: models for language, code for facts. A model is good at reading messy wording. It is not a safe source for deadlines or officer names, so those never come from a model.

The model may only choose one type from a fixed list. Every day count, owner, escalation step and reply sentence comes from the charter file through plain tools. A wrong answer is rejected and the keyword rules are used.
