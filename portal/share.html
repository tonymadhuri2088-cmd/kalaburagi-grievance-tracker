Try all three roles, on three devices

Each person uses their own device. The three screens talk to the same server, so a complaint filed on one appears on the others straight away.

Phone 1

Citizen

  1. Open the link and choose Sign up. Enter a name, a mobile number and an address in Kalaburagi.
  2. Log in with the 6-digit code.
  3. Register Complaint: pick the category, add 3 photos, describe the problem.
  4. Track Complaint shows each step, the time limit and the reminders.
Open citizen sign up
Phone 2

Worker

  1. Open Worker sign up: name, mobile number, address, ward and department.
  2. Log in with the 6-digit code.
  3. Accept a registered complaint, do the work, then ask for a completion code.
  4. The citizen must share their code before it is closed.
Open worker sign up
Laptop

Officer

  1. Open the portal and choose the Officer Login tab.
  2. Use a demo account from the table below. The team gives you the password.
  3. See the complaints for your department, reminders under Notices, and reply or escalate.
  4. district.head sees the whole district.

Install it like an app

Open the link on a phone. On Android, tap Install as an app (or the browser menu, then Install app). On iPhone, open it in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. It gets its own icon and opens full screen, with no browser bar. Nothing to download from a store.
